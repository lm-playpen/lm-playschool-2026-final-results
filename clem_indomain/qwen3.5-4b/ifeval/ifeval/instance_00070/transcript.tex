\texttt{\#\# 🕰️ Session I: The Dawn of the Sun (Introduction \& History)} \\
\texttt{*Time: 09:00 – 10:00*} \\
\\ 
\texttt{**The Grand Opening Ceremony**} \\
\texttt{We shall begin not with a lecture, but with a symphony of history. We will stand before the altar of the Java Virtual Machine (JVM) and witness the birth of the "Write Once, Run Anywhere" prophecy.} \\
\texttt{*   **The Oracle of Origins:** A visual feast detailing James Gosling's journey from green tomatoes to the global giant. We shall analyze the architecture of the original 1995 release versus the modern, cloud{-}native behemoth of today.} \\
\texttt{*   **The Platform of Possibility:** We shall explore the JVM not as a machine, but as a magical, universal translator that speaks to every operating system in the multiverse, from the ancient mainframes of the 80s to the quantum computers of tomorrow.} \\

\texttt{*   Participants will write their very first "Hello, World!" not on paper, but projected onto the digital heavens.} \\
\texttt{*   **Ritual:** We shall compile and run this code together, watching the compiler transform human intent into binary magic, and the JVM breathe life into it.} \\

\texttt{\#\# 🕰️ Session II: The Architecture of the Soul (Core Syntax \& OOP)} \\
\texttt{*Time: 10:00 – 12:00*} \\
\\ 
\texttt{**The Pillars of Object{-}Oriented Design**} \\
\texttt{Here, we shall construct the very soul of our application. We shall treat classes as temples and objects as the divine manifestations within them.} \\
\texttt{*   **The Class Blueprint:** We shall draw the blueprints of our objects, defining their attributes (the essence) and their behaviors (the actions).} \\
\texttt{*   **The Inheritance of Wisdom:** We shall learn how to build upon the shoulders of giants, creating hierarchies of knowledge where a `Dog` inherits the wisdom of the `Animal` and adds its own unique barking capabilities.} \\
\texttt{*   **Polymorphism of the Gods:** We shall witness the magic of duck typing and method overriding, where a single command can yield different, yet perfectly harmonious, results depending on the object's nature.} \\
\\ 
\texttt{**Activity: The Castle of Classes**} \\
\texttt{*   In groups, participants will design a "Castle of Classes" representing a futuristic city. Each group member will define a unique class (e.g., `SkyScraper`, `TrafficLight`, `Cloud`) and demonstrate how they interact through inheritance and polymorphism.} \\
\\ 
\texttt{\#\# 🕰️ Session III: The Alchemy of Data (Collections \& Streams)} \\
\texttt{*Time: 13:00 – 15:00*} \\
\\ 
\texttt{**The Library of Collections**} \\
\texttt{Data is the fuel of our empire. We shall explore the vast libraries of `ArrayList`, `HashMap`, and `Set`, treating them as magical vaults where data can be stored, retrieved, and organized with effortless grace.} \\
\texttt{*   **The Map of Memories:** We shall master the `HashMap`, learning how to associate keys with values in a way that is as fast as lightning and as reliable as the sun.} \\
\texttt{*   **The Stream of Consciousness:** We shall introduce the modern Java Streams API, transforming the tedious loops of the past into elegant, declarative pipelines of data transformation.} \\
\\ 
\texttt{**Activity: The Data Transmutation**} \\
\texttt{*   Participants will be given a chaotic stream of raw data (e.g., a list of planets with their distances from the sun). They must use Streams to filter, sort, and calculate the total distance of all planets in a single, fluid operation, demonstrating the power of functional programming.} \\
\\ 
\texttt{\#\# 🕰️ Session IV: The Fortress of Security (Concurrency \& Exception Handling)} \\
\texttt{*Time: 15:00 – 16:30*} \\
\\ 
\texttt{**The Concurrency of the Multiverse**} \\
\texttt{In a world of infinite threads, we shall learn to orchestrate chaos into harmony. We shall master `Threads`, `Executors`, and `Futures`, allowing our applications to perform multiple tasks simultaneously with the precision of a Swiss watch.} \\
\texttt{*   **The Race Condition:** We shall simulate a race condition and learn how to build the "synchronization gates" that keep our data safe from the chaos of parallel execution.} \\
\texttt{*   **The Exception of Destiny:** We shall learn to anticipate the errors of the universe. We shall wrap our code in `try{-}catch{-}finally` blocks, ensuring that even if the world ends, our application handles the crash with dignity and logs the tragedy for future reference.} \\
\\ 
\texttt{**Activity: The Parallel Universe**} \\
\texttt{*   A high{-}stakes simulation where participants must calculate the factorial of a massive number using multiple threads. They will race to see who can achieve the most accurate result with the least amount of "race condition" chaos.} \\
\\ 
\texttt{\#\# 🕰️ Session V: The Final Coronation (Project \& Reflection)} \\
\texttt{*Time: 16:30 – 17:30*} \\

\texttt{**The Grand Project: The Solar System Simulator**} \\

\texttt{    *   Inheritance for planets and stars.} \\
\texttt{    *   Collections to store orbital data.} \\
\texttt{    *   Streams to calculate total system mass.} \\
\texttt{    *   Concurrency to simulate multiple satellites orbiting simultaneously.} \\
\texttt{    *   Exception handling to manage user input errors gracefully.} \\
