\texttt{    *   Logical form: \$\textbackslash{}neg \textbackslash{}forall r (R(r) \textbackslash{}to S(r))\$} \\

\texttt{3.  **Conclusion:** "Somebody is a sister of Ruth and not a classmate of Marisa."} \\
\texttt{    *   Logical form: \$\textbackslash{}exists x (R(x) \textbackslash{}land \textbackslash{}neg C(x))\$.} \\

\texttt{*   From Premise 2, we know there is a specific individual (let's call her \$a\$) who is a sister of Ruth (\$R(a)\$) and is not a schoolmate of Karen (\$\textbackslash{}neg S(a)\$).} \\
