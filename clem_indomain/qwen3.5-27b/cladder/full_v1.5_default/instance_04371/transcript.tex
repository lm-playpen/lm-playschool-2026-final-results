\texttt{**1. Analyze the Causal Structure**} \\
\texttt{The problem describes the following causal graph:} \\
\texttt{*   \$Gender \textbackslash{}to Smoking\$} \\
\texttt{*   \$Gender \textbackslash{}to PeanutAllergy\$} \\
\texttt{*   \$Smoking \textbackslash{}to TarDeposit\$} \\
\texttt{*   \$TarDeposit \textbackslash{}to PeanutAllergy\$} \\

\texttt{Here, \$Gender\$ is an unobserved confounder between \$Smoking\$ and \$PeanutAllergy\$. However, the question asks for the causal effect of \$Smoking\$ on \$PeanutAllergy\$. In the \$do\${-}calculus framework, intervening on \$Smoking\$ (\$do(S)\$) breaks the incoming arrow from \$Gender\$ to \$Smoking\$. Therefore, the distribution of \$Gender\$ does not influence the post{-}intervention distribution of \$Smoking\$ (since we force \$S\$ to be 1 or 0), but we must account for the path \$Smoking \textbackslash{}to TarDeposit \textbackslash{}to PeanutAllergy\$.} \\

\texttt{Crucially, the problem provides conditional probabilities of \$PeanutAllergy\$ given \$Smoking\$ and \$TarDeposit\$. Let \$Y\$ be Peanut Allergy, \$S\$ be Smoking, and \$T\$ be Tar Deposit.} \\
\texttt{The provided data gives us \$P(Y=1 | S, T)\$.} \\
\texttt{*   \$P(Y=1 | S=0, T=0) = 0.49\$} \\

\texttt{**2. Calculate the Counterfactual Probabilities**} \\
\texttt{Since \$TarDeposit\$ is a mediator on the causal path from \$Smoking\$ to \$PeanutAllergy\$, and there are no other paths from \$Smoking\$ to \$PeanutAllergy\$ (the direct path \$Gender \textbackslash{}to PeanutAllergy\$ is blocked by the intervention on \$Smoking\$ which severs the \$Gender \textbackslash{}to Smoking\$ link, and \$Gender\$ does not affect \$TarDeposit\$ directly), we can calculate the interventional probability using the law of total probability over the mediator \$T\$.} \\

\texttt{Under this intervention, the probability of Tar Deposit is determined by \$P(T|S=0)\$.} \\

\texttt{*   Probability of allergy if forced to smoke: **0.473**} \\
