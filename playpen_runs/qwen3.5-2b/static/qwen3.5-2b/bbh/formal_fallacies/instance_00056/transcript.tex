\texttt{Let \$C(x)\$ mean "\$x\$ is a classmate of Sal".} \\
\texttt{Let \$S(x)\$ mean "\$x\$ is a son of Ronald".} \\

\texttt{**2. Translate the Premises:**} \\
\texttt{*   **Premise 1:** "Everyone who is a half{-}brother of Ronny or a classmate of Sal is not a son of Ronald."} \\
\texttt{    *   This can be written as: \$\textbackslash{}forall x ( (H(x) \textbackslash{}lor C(x)) \textbackslash{}implies \textbackslash{}neg S(x) )\$.} \\
\texttt{    *   *Logical Equivalent:* If someone is a half{-}brother or a classmate, they cannot be a son.} \\
\texttt{*   **Premise 2:** "It is false that Santo is a son of Ronald."} \\
\texttt{    *   This translates to: \$\textbackslash{}neg S(\textbackslash{}text\{Santo\})\$.} \\
\\ 
\texttt{**3. Analyze the Conclusion:**} \\
\texttt{*   **Conclusion:** "Santo is a half{-}brother of Ronny or a classmate of Sal."} \\
\texttt{    *   Symbolically: \$H(\textbackslash{}text\{Santo\}) \textbackslash{}lor C(\textbackslash{}text\{Santo\})\$.} \\
\\ 
\texttt{**4. Test for Validity:**} \\
\texttt{An argument is valid if and only if it is impossible for the premises to be true while the conclusion is false. In other words, if the premises are true, the conclusion *must* be true.} \\
\\ 
\texttt{*   From Premise 2, we know \$\textbackslash{}neg S(\textbackslash{}text\{Santo\})\$ is true.} \\
\texttt{*   From Premise 1, we know that for any person \$x\$, if \$S(x)\$ is true, then \$(H(x) \textbackslash{}lor C(x))\$ is false.} \\
\texttt{*   By the rule of **Modus Tollens** (if \$P \textbackslash{}implies Q\$ and \$\textbackslash{}neg Q\$, then \$\textbackslash{}neg P\$):} \\
\texttt{    *   Let \$P = S(\textbackslash{}text\{Santo\})\$ and \$Q = (H(\textbackslash{}text\{Santo\}) \textbackslash{}lor C(\textbackslash{}text\{Santo\}))\$.} \\
\texttt{    *   We have \$S(\textbackslash{}text\{Santo\}) \textbackslash{}implies (H(\textbackslash{}text\{Santo\}) \textbackslash{}lor C(\textbackslash{}text\{Santo\}))\$.} \\
\texttt{    *   We know \$\textbackslash{}neg S(\textbackslash{}text\{Santo\})\$ is true.} \\
\texttt{    *   Therefore, \$\textbackslash{}neg (H(\textbackslash{}text\{Santo\}) \textbackslash{}lor C(\textbackslash{}text\{Santo\}))\$ must be true.} \\
\texttt{    *   This means it is **false** that Santo is a half{-}brother or a classmate.} \\
