The infamous slipperiness of a banana peel is often blamed on dirt or wax, but the biology is actually quite fascinating. This **slippery surface** is an evolutionary trade-off; while it helps the fruit slide down the stalk for better air circulation, it also makes the ripe fruit easy to detach.
